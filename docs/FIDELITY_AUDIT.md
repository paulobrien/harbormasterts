# Harbor Master HD fidelity audit

## Scope and method

This document tracks what can be established from the supplied **Harbor Master HD 2.0.1** iPad IPA and what remains uncertain. The IPA contains an ARMv7 32-bit Mach-O executable, not the original source code. Its symbol and debug tables retain useful names, paths and addresses. We inventoried these and inspected individual functions using ARM disassembly and compared their observable effects with the TypeScript implementation and the supplied level data. Original assets and executable are user-supplied and are not included in this repository.

The executable contains 27,240 symbol/debug records and references 159 distinct compilation-unit paths under the original project. These include third-party advertising, purchase, social, network, archive, rendering and audio libraries, in addition to the game. A symbol record is not a source line, function, or independently verified behavior. The inventory is broad; it is **not an exhaustive function-by-function decompilation** or a proof that every behavior is understood. Equivalent-looking math and game feel still require side-by-side comparison with a running original.

The supplied HD IPA includes the Treasure Island map and its assets. Its in-app purchase UI and other map-specific game classes are present, but the other HD map files themselves are not in this IPA. Their behavior cannot be fully exercised or reproduced from this copy alone. Online services, multiplayer, advertisements, purchases, leaderboards and the original operating system integrations were not recreated or tested as part of this single-player map audit.

## What we inspected

- `cBoat` constructor, path, motion and simulation; `cGameController` input, pacing, spawning, collisions, auto-docking, scoring and game-state methods; `cDock` and level data.
- `cLand` and `cRock` distinctions, dock and land coordinate systems, and the supplied level's collision polygons, pacing records and dock settings.
- User-interface and localization resources, audio files and named methods for preferences, records, high scores, saving and loading.
- Existing browser gameplay, importer, persistence, controls and regression tests.

The current implementation is an independent recreation. It loads the map, graphics and audio from a separately staged game-data bundle, or can import them from an IPA selected by the player if needed. A playable deployment serves its staged bundle to browsers; scores and saved shifts remain on the device. The repository does not include original assets. The app is not running or translating the original executable.

## Evidence and coverage

| Area | Original evidence | Current status / confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Map geometry | `hmhd0001.lvl` specifies a 1024 × 768 map, six docks and three land polygons. Land coordinates are centered with Y upwards; dock Y uses the bottom-left origin. | The importer reads the player's original level. Regression checks exercise the map's dock approaches and departures. Exact rendering and collision geometry remain subject to visual/gameplay comparison. |
| Boat sizes and motion | `cBoat` constructor (`0x5134`) configures maximum speeds of 40, 20 and 10 game units/s for small, medium and large boats. `handleTurn` (`0x3470`) has distinct angular limits. During path following, `cBoat::simulate` (`0x638c`) advances position along path segments using a speed × time budget, while rotating the visible orientation separately. `spawnBoat` (`0xd4d8`) assigns capacities of 1, 2 and 4 cargo items. | The recreation uses these speeds, turn limits and capacities, traverses drawn path segments by distance and uses the verified sprite regions. Exact turning and collision outlines remain subject to comparison against a running original. |
| Routes | `cBoat::handleTouchMoved` samples points at distances above 9 units and invokes its curve routine for gaps of at least 100 units. `cBoat::addPathPointsUsingCurve` (`0x3b70`) invokes an eight-sample curve helper named `cBSpline3D::InBetween` (`0x4bf84`); inspection identifies cubic Bézier-like interpolation. `cGameController::handleTouchMoved` (`0xebb0`) checks drawn segments against land. | Simultaneous touch, gap sampling, cubic interpolation and shoreline clipping are implemented. Exact curve handles, docking paths and touch responsiveness need side-by-side validation. |
| Land and rocks | `landElement` corresponds to `cLand` and `rockElement` to a separate `cRock`. The boat simulation reflects boats from land and clears the route; rock collision can end the shift. Treasure Island has no `rockElement`. | Shoreline reflection and route clearing are implemented; the exact native collision and exemption details remain under review. There is no test of original rock hazards on other levels. |
| Screen edge | Native boat simulation reflects loaded boats back from a screen edge, allows unloaded boats to leave, and treats new arrivals separately. | Implemented and tested for all four edges, but exact spawn-entry and reflection details are approximate. |
| Docks and score | Native `handleTouchMoved` and `handleAutoDocking` (`0x8fa4`) contain distinct path-assisted and automatic docking cases. `cDock` includes occupancy, blocked state, cargo types and unload-speed factor. Unloading scores one point per cargo item, with a two-second base divided by the dock speed factor. | Imported docks, cargo scoring, deliberate and automatic docking, warning exclusion and an Auto Docking preference are implemented. Exact curved final approaches and edge cases require further verification. |
| Pacing and arrivals | `handlePacing` (`0xe708`) and `spawnBoat` (`0xd4d8`) manage stress, boat sizes, timing, spawn positions and obstruction checks. The original selects uniformly among available boat types and checks randomized entries at all four screen edges. The initial heading points toward the map center and the shoreline check covers the first 30 units inside the edge. | The level settings, available-type selection, four-edge spawn and pressure-state model are integrated and covered by synthetic and HD-level tests. Warning detection, obstruction checks and exact probability distributions remain approximations; original gameplay traces would help validate difficulty. |
| Music and effects | The IPA contains a 54-second MP3 and CAF effects; the audio subsystem and preferences are named in the binary. | Audio is imported locally and played; separate music and effects volume settings persist on the device. Exact original mixing, timing, transitions and background behavior require additional comparison. |
| End-of-shift, records and rewind | Native `saveGame` (`0x9300`), `loadGame` (`0x1d314`), `setHighScore` (`0x14730`), `canRewind` (`0x854c`) and `rewind` (`0x8d58`) exist. Rewind checks and consumes a player's available rewind, removes crashed/warning/under-attack boats and some hazard objects; it should not be assumed to be a generic time-travel snapshot. | Local records and resuming in-progress shifts are implemented with browser-local state. Original profile/achievement menus and rewind are not implemented. |
| Non-Treasure Island modes | The binary contains classes and methods for cannons, pirates, cyclones, monsters, additional maps, and Bluetooth multiplayer. | They are not covered by this single-map implementation; absent HD assets and unavailable services limit verification. |

## Verification and remaining work

Current automated regression tests exercise movement, routing, land and screen-edge behavior, scoring, collision, docking and multitouch. Independent tests used the user's actual map geometry locally to check all six dock approaches and departures; original assets were not added to the repository. Tests prove those scenarios against our implementation, not semantic equivalence to the original app.

Priorities for a closer recreation are: validate path interpolation and turning against recorded original gameplay; compare pressure pacing, warning detection and docking to the original; and test audio, controls, restoration and visual timing on the target Android device. A broader project could implement original rewind semantics, then investigate additional locally supplied HD map assets if available.
